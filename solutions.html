Strategic Freight Engineering

Intelligent Supply Chain Solutions

Beyond moving individual pallets, we architect resilient multimodal networks that protect working capital, accelerate transit velocity, and mitigate global trade disruption.

Multimodal Network Architecture

Relying on a single mode creates vulnerability. We engineer hybrid transport plans—combining deep-sea container sailings with electrified inland rail corridors and dedicated final-mile road dispatch. This achieves up to 40% cost savings over air freight while trimming up to 10 days off standard ocean transit.

Time-Critical & Hotshot Response

When an aircraft is on the ground (AOG) or a factory assembly line is minutes from stalling, standard schedules do not suffice. NEXORA activates 24/7 emergency dispatch desks, securing direct-drive vans, on-board couriers (OBC), and priority air charters with guaranteed ramp recovery.

Dedicated Direct Drive Ramp Priority Offload Emergency Dispatch

Cross-Border Customs Engineering

Regulatory compliance errors cause over 65% of international cargo holds. We proactively audit commercial documentation, align Harmonized System (HS) codes, and arrange bonded transit corridors (T1/Carnet) so shipments clear borders before physical arrival.

Pre-Clearance Filing Tariff Classification Demurrage Prevention

Carbon-Conscious Logistics Routing

Modern enterprises are accountable for Scope 3 emissions. We help shippers shift eligible arterial road freight onto electrified rail networks, consolidate LTL shipments into maximum-density full trailers, and provide transparent CO₂ emission reporting per route.
