Transportation & Logistics Services

From scheduled full-truckload corridors to transatlantic priority air cargo and high-volume container sea freight, our operations deliver predictability at every milestone.

Corridor Operations

Road Freight (FTL & LTL)

Our road freight network operates across core industrial corridors, connecting manufacturing plants, distribution centers, and commercial hubs. We provide dedicated full-truckload (FTL) capacity as well as consolidated less-than-truckload (LTL) services.

Shipment Types: Standard Euro/Standard pallets, industrial crated equipment, non-perishable consumer freight.
Key Use Cases: Cross-border arterial replenishment, factory-to-warehouse transfers, project machinery delivery.
Considerations: CMR documentation, driver hours regulations, route transit permit compliance.

Time-Definite Air Cargo

Air Freight & Charter Services

When time-to-market or critical assembly deadlines demand speed, NEXORA coordinates priority air freight across international gateways. We manage cargo loading, airport apron handling, and customs pre-clearance.

Shipment Types: High-value technology, aerospace replacement parts, critical diagnostic hardware, automotive urgent freight.
Key Use Cases: Assembly line shutdown avoidance, critical product launches, emergency maintenance replenishment.
Considerations: IATA volumetric weight ratios (1:6000), air security X-ray screening, lithium battery regulatory declarations.

Deepwater Maritime

Ocean Freight (FCL & LCL)

Ocean freight remains the economic backbone of international trade. NEXORA secures container allocations on premier shipping lines, coordinating full container loads (20ft, 40ft, 40ft High Cube) and less-than-container consolidation boxes.

Shipment Types: Standard ISO shipping containers, dry van, open-top, palletized high-density manufacturing cargo.
Key Use Cases: Seasonal inventory stocking, bulk wholesale importation, intercontinental manufacturing raw material flows.
Considerations: Port dwell times, demurrage/detention windows, VGM (Verified Gross Mass) container weighing requirements.

Inland Corridors

Rail Freight & Intermodal

Rail corridors offer high-capacity, weather-resilient transport connecting coastal ports directly to inland container terminals. Intermodal container transfers minimize highway congestion and substantially reduce supply chain carbon emissions.

Shipment Types: Intermodal container trains, heavy bulk freight, standardized cargo wagons.
Key Use Cases: Port-to-inland distribution, cross-continental land bridges, heavy industrial commodity movements.
Considerations: Terminal transfer scheduling, rail gauge transitions, wagon availability.

Facility Coordination

Warehousing & Strategic Storage

Buffer inventory and consolidate shipments close to your end customers. NEXORA coordinates multi-client pallet racking, cross-docking facilities, carton-level picking, and scheduled dispatch staging.

Capabilities: High-bay pallet racking, cross-dock consolidation, serial number tracking, pallet reworking.
Key Use Cases: Regional distribution hubs, production inventory buffers, seasonal e-commerce order staging.
Considerations: Minimum pallet volume commitments, inbound ASN documentation, outbound dispatch cutoffs.

Compliance & Declarations

Customs Coordination & Documentation

International border crossings require rigorous compliance to avoid costly port storage and inspection holds. We provide structured customs paperwork coordination, harmonized tariff code assistance, and bonded transit support.

Services: Import/export declaration prep, T1 transit bonds, commercial invoice audits, certificate of origin checks.
Important Notice: NEXORA acts as an operational logistics coordinator. Customs brokerage licenses and statutory tax clearances are executed through certified local customs partners.
