Privacy Policy & Data Standards

NEXORA LOGISTICS maintains strict confidentiality protocols for all corporate freight specifications, routing contracts, and commercial inquiries.

EFFECTIVE DATE: OCTOBER 2026 | VERSION 2.1

1. Information We Collect

When you request a quotation or submit an operational inquiry via this portal, we collect business contact details (name, corporate email, phone number, company name) and cargo specifications (origin, destination, weight, dimensions, commodity type). We do not collect consumer payment card details on this website.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

Collected business data is utilized strictly to:
• Calculate freight routing options and prepare commercial rate proposals;
• Coordinate with terminal operators, customs agents, and carriers for confirmed shipments;
• Provide milestone tracking alerts and operational updates.

3. Telematics & Tracking Queries

Reference IDs entered into our tracking portal (e.g. NEX-849201) are processed to retrieve current milestone checkpoints. We do not store personal tracking session profiles or sell shipment telematics to third-party data aggregators.

4. Demonstration Platform Status

This website serves as a live digital demonstration interface. Sample quotation reference codes generated in client demonstration mode remain stored in local browser memory and are not transmitted to third-party external networks unless a backend webhook is explicitly configured by the site administrator.

5. Contacting the Data Protection Officer

For inquiries regarding corporate data privacy, information deletion requests, or NDA execution, contact our compliance desk at privacy@nexora-logistics.demo.
