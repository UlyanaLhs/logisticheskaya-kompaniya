Frequently Asked Questions

Common Logistics Questions

Essential operational details regarding quotations, billing weight, customs documentation, and transit monitoring.

Freight carriers charge based on whichever is greater: the actual physical weight (gross weight) or the space the cargo occupies in the transport vehicle (volumetric/chargeable weight). In air cargo, 1 cubic meter (CBM) equals 167 kg (standard IATA 1:6000 ratio). On road freight, 1 CBM typically equals 333 kg. Our online quote calculator automatically evaluates dimensional weight.
No. Online quotations submitted through this portal represent preliminary operational estimates based on stated dimensions and current market fuel surcharges. A legally binding freight agreement is finalized only after carrier space confirmation, cargo documentation verification, and dispatch booking confirmation.
Standard cross-border shipments require a Commercial Invoice detailing cargo valuation, a detailed Packing List specifying carton/pallet piece counts and gross/net weights, and the primary transport document (CMR for road, Air Waybill for air, or Bill of Lading for sea). Depending on the commodity, certificates of origin or export control declarations may also be required.
Every shipment is assigned a unique NEXORA reference identifier (e.g. NEX-849201). You can enter this ID into our Shipment Tracking portal at any time to review milestone checkpoints, customs clearances, and estimated hub arrivals.
