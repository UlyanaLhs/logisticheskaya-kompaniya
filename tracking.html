Enterprise Telematics Architecture

Connecting Live Telematics & Carriers

How NEXORA connects with production freight management platforms and transportation management systems (TMS).

1. Carrier Aggregator Feeds

Connect multi-carrier visibility providers like Project44, FourKites, or Shippeo. The backend normalizes AIS vessel data, ELD truck telematics, and airline flight numbers into unified REST endpoints.

2. EDI 214 & Webhook Events

Support traditional ANSI X12 EDI 214 (Transportation Carrier Shipment Status Message) and modern JSON webhook triggers dispatched whenever a container gate-in or terminal departure is logged.
